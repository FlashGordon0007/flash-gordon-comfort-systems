Meet Your Designer

Jennifer KudiJaroff
Color is her language.

Jennifer doesn't have a signature style — she has a signature process. She starts with what you love most. A painting. A fabric. A memory. Then she builds your entire space around it, pulling colors, textures, and light until your home feels like it was always meant to look exactly this way.

She has designed $100,000 kitchens and $15,000 kitchens. Both look like they belong in a magazine. The difference is never the budget — it's the eye and comes down to the details that high quality materials last longer and can take more punishment.

Jennifer is a master of lighting design. Under-counter, toe-kick, accent, art lighting, dimmers — she thinks about every bulb and every shadow. She'll go shopping with you. She'll hold the swatch up to your wall at 3pm and then again at 7pm. She will shop online like a bloodhound and won't sign off until it's right.

What We Do

Fast turnarounds.
Unforgettable results.

We focus on high-impact transformations that can be designed and built in days, not months. Because life is too short to live with a bathroom you don't love.

01

Curbless Shower Transformation

From demo to done in 5–10 days. We remove the curb, set a custom-sloped mud bed, lay large-format tile, and install frameless glass. The result looks like a high-end hotel — forever.

02

Bathroom Facelift

New vanity, new fixtures, new tile, new lighting — all coordinated by Jennifer. We can transform the look of a bathroom in under two weeks without touching the plumbing rough-in.

03

Designer Lighting Upgrade

Under-counter, toe-kick, accent, art, and recessed lighting on smart dimmers installed by a master electrician. We use the LeGrand Adorne collection — the detail that makes visitors stop and ask questions.

04

Full Master Bath Renovation

Full custom renovation projects — complete design and build. Freestanding soakers, curbless walk-in showers, matched marble floor-to-ceiling, custom vanities, custome built cabinets, from the hardware to the glass. Two to four weeks start to finish.
