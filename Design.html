Meet Your Designer

Jennifer Kudijaroff
Color is her language.

Jennifer doesn't have a signature style — she has a signature process. She starts with what you love most. A painting. A fabric. A memory of a place that made you feel something. Then she builds your entire space around it, pulling colors, textures, and light until your home feels like it was always meant to look exactly this way.

She has designed $100,000 kitchens and $15,000 kitchens. Both look like they belong in a magazine. The difference is never the budget — it's the eye and comes down to the details that high quality materials last longer and can take more punishment. Jennifer knows where to invest and where to be clever. She'll specify a stunning mitered quartz countertop, then find the perfect fixture on Wayfair. She'll use flat-pack cabinetry and make it look completely custom. Every decision serves the whole.

Jennifer is a master of lighting design. Under-counter, toe-kick, accent, art lighting, dimmers — she thinks about every bulb and every shadow. She'll go shopping with you. She'll hold the swatch up to your wall at 3pm and then again at 7pm. She will shop online like a bloodhound and won't sign off until it's right.

Signature Lighting Design

Light isn't an afterthought.
It's the whole story.

Most contractors install light fixtures. Jennifer designs light experiences. Before a single tile is cut, she's already planning every layer — the warm glow of under-counter strips, the soft theater of toe-kick lighting at midnight, the art-grade beam that makes a painting look like it's lit from within.

She once specified a chandelier made entirely of hand-blown solid glass birds. When you turned the birds on their stems, they cast shadow-play patterns across the walls — sunlight and silhouette shifting with the angle. It wasn't a light fixture. It was a mood generator.

Every project uses the LeGrand Adorne collection — the most beautifully designed switch and outlet system on the market. Dimmers on every circuit that warrants it. Recessed lighting positioned not just for brightness, but for shadow. Smart home integration where it matters.

We do ceiling-mount waterfall shower fixtures that become the centerpiece of the room. We do cove lighting above cabinets. We do accent lighting inside display niches. We do everything that makes 7pm in your bathroom feel like a five-star hotel.

Signature Feature

The fireplace that
defines the room.

We are the authorized Flare Modern Fireplaces dealer for the Bellingham and Eastside markets. Linear, see-through, single-sided — Flare fireplaces are architectural statements, not afterthoughts.

Jennifer designs around them. She'll plan the tile surround, the hearth material, the mantle profile, and the accent lighting so the fireplace reads as a complete composition — not just a box in a wall. When the fire is off, it's a sculpture. When it's on, it's the entire room.

We handle full installation — gas line, venting, surround, and smart home integration so you can set the flame height from your phone.
